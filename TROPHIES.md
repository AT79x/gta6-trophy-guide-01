# GTA VI trophies — verification notes

How this guide's trophy list was checked, what is confirmed, what is not,
and where each fact comes from. Written 19 November 2026, the day GTA VI
launched (19 Nov 2026).

## The list

- **55 trophies on PlayStation 5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze.
  28 are hidden** until earned. The same 55 exist on Xbox Series X|S as
  achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore in total.
- **Platinum: “Vice Got a Price!”** — “Congratulations! You're a Leonida
  Legend.” (earn every other trophy).
- **Golds: “A Long Road”** (attain 100% completion) and **“You Gotta Know
  When to Get Off”** (complete the final mission).
- **Silvers (6):** Cunning Stunts (250 stunts), The Shootist (gold in all 42
  Shooting Range challenges), Strangers & Freaks (8 stranger strands),
  Don't Touch My Deck (3 Collectible Card sets), You WAiNKer! (WAiNK It at
  100 locations), Maximum Withdrawal (rob 5 bank vaults and escape).
- The kit's starter `app/data/trophies.json` (as handed out, from Exophase
  and PSNProfiles, 18 Nov 2026) agrees on all 55 names, descriptions and
  grades with the public guides below. This guide keeps those official
  names and descriptions verbatim and adds its own guides, sources, kinds,
  missable flags and roadmap stages.

## The one missable trophy

**Positive Mental Attitude** (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the
only trophy reported as possibly missable, and every source above agrees:

- Lucia's first meditation happens in **Chapter 1**, at Jason's house: after
  the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning;
  she says she is going to meditate, walks to the balcony, and the player
  follows and accepts. It disappears after Chapter 1.
- **There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus**, so the safe play is
  a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final
  mission.
- Whether that balcony meditation is strictly required for the trophy is
  **still unconfirmed** (PowerPyx, Kengamer and Output Lag all say so);
  PowerPyx's author earned the trophy after doing it. This guide tells the
  player to do it anyway.
- Meditation spots: **18 spots in 10 locations** (PowerPyx, GTABase).
  Meditating with both Jason and Lucia at one spot counts twice. Track
  under Progress → Collections → Exploration → Meditations.
- Note the count discrepancy: the 100% completion checklist asks for only
  15 of the 18 spots, so the trophy and the checklist count different
  things. This guide reports both numbers instead of picking one.

## Other confirmed facts

- **No online trophies; no difficulty trophies; no mission medals.** All
  55 are single-player and offline.
- **~200 hours** for the platinum (PowerPyx, Output Lag). It is long, not
  hard.
- Cheats switch off trophies and saving (Output Lag).
- Story structure in the official descriptions: opening mission Gone
  Fishing, Chapters 1–4 (“Paradise Lost”, “T**s Ass Drug Money”,
  “Vice City, Baby!”, “Top Of The World, Ma”), final mission, named
  missions Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners,
  VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory, and side-character finales for
  Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus Bret's hunt.
- Counts in the official descriptions: 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range
  challenges (gold), 14 Top Fun Drone Race locations, 20 races, 9 activity
  types, 8 stranger strands, 18 meditation spots, 3 card sets, WAiNK It at
  100 locations, 50 challenges, 100 vehicles, 5 bank vaults, 52 areas of
  Leonida, 9 gangs' Illegal Operations, $250,000 laundering Marked Bills,
  $100,000 spent in shops, 8 garage properties, 10 vehicles in the
  Scooter Bros app.

## Discrepancies and unknowns (stated, not hidden)

- An older datamine leak named the platinum **“Vice City Legend”**. The
  published list (Exophase/PSNProfiles, Push Square, PowerPyx, Kengamer,
  Output Lag) names it **“Vice Got a Price!”** — the published list wins.
- Rockstar itself had not published an official trophy list before launch;
  the list comes from the trackers and press guides above, which all agree.
- The exact locations of all 18 meditation spots and all Collectible Card
  sets were still being mapped by guide sites at launch. Where a guide in
  this entry cannot name a confirmed location, it says so and points to
  PowerPyx/GTABase rather than inventing one.
- Two trophy descriptions are deliberately vague (“Personal Best”,
  “The Truth Is Out There”); their guides say the trigger is still being
  confirmed instead of guessing.
- The hidden-trophy split (28 hidden, 27 visible) is from the published
  lists; which individual trophies PlayStation marks hidden is the kit
  starter's assignment (story and secret trophies hidden), kept as-is.
- “Solmar Bay” is the fictional city of a separate make.fun GTA 6 game
  entry by the same author. It is **not** the real game's map — the real
  game is set in Leonida, centred on Vice City — and it appears nowhere
  in this guide as a real location.
