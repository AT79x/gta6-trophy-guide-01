⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter, and GTA VI has no mission replay. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one before the final mission. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story.

Sources: PowerPyx, Kengamer, Output Lag — see the trophy's guide below.
